# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in Inkify a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and users.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual is representing the project publicly.
